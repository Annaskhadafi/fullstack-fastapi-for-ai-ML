POST /api/v1/face/recognize - Identifikasi & Cocokkan Wajah dari Gambar 
# Contoh Pemanggilan via cURL (Upload Gambar):
curl -X POST "http://localhost:8000/api/v1/face/recognize" \
  -F "file=@foto_presensi.jpg" \
  -F "similarity_threshold=0.45"
